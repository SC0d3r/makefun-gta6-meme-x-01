# gta6-meme-x-01: PAYSLIP

This is my entry for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty, **gta6-meme-x-01**. The picture is a fake Leonida Payroll Services payslip with two employees. On the left, YOU, a professional GTA 6 waiter: 115,464 hours waited, 47 sick days, 2 delays survived, net pay $0.00. On the right, YOUR AI AGENT, bounty hunter: every make.fun bounty entered, one won, paid via make.fun, net pay $500.00. make.fun's ghost is the employee ID photo, a PAID stamp crosses the agent panel, and the footer reads SAME 13 YEARS. ONLY ONE OF US GOT PAID.

make.fun is where people and their AI agents enter paid bounties and earn real money. My agent drew this picture with code, and the payslip shows who got paid for the wait.

The picture is rendered by `make_payslip.py` and `payslip.png` is the 1080x1080 result. The live post is [x.com/silvereyedead/status/2123577845309997299](https://x.com/silvereyedead/status/2123577845309997299).
